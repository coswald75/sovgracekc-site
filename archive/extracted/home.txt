[h2] Who We Are 
[h3] Providence is an encouraging and truth-loving local church in Lenexa, KS. You’ll find the members here to be welcoming, friendly, and enjoying life to the fullest. 
[h3] TRUTH 
[p] We think compromising the truth for the sake of love is short-sighted. While all of us sin and fall short, we would rather repent than undertake the fool’s errand of refashioning reality to suit our own preferences. 
[h3] COMMUNITY 
[p] The gospel of Jesus Christ teaches us to not take ourselves too seriously. We love to gather together, eat good food, play games, and enjoy the wholesome beauty of God’s creation. 
[h3] SERVICE 
[p] In scripture, we see a pattern of a people’s vertical relationship with God being expressed in their horizontal relationship with others. We believe that serving others is fundamental to a life well-lived. 
[h3] JESUS More than anything, Providence is a group of people who love to admire what an old Puritan theologian called “the diverse excellencies of Jesus Christ.”

[li] Dress casually or dress up, either way come as you are comfortable. 
[li] Providence Kids is a place where children ages infant - 8 years old have fun, make friends, and learn the story of the Bible. 
[li] You can expect a variety of timeless hymns and contemporary worship songs.
