[h1] We All Have Questions 
[h2] The Bridge Course is a way to explore the Christian faith in a relaxed, non-threatening environment over ten weeks. Register for the Course 
[h1] Conversations about the Big Questions of Life 
[h2] Why does God allow suffering? Is Jesus the only way? Is Christianity really worth it? Each week there is a 30 minute talk designed to raise important questions and inspire conversation. After this, there’s a 30 minute discussion to interact with the talk and share your perspective. It’s a safe environment where you can ask whatever question you like or not say a word. No one will ever call on you. And the whole thing is free! Register for the Course 
[h1] Here's How it Works 
[h1] First up: Food 
[h2] Food has a way of bringing us together and creating good conversation. We start with food so we can get to know each other! 
[h1] Next: Talk 
[h2] Talks are designed to raise important questions and inspire conversation. Generally thirty minutes long, they seek to answer important questions. 
[h1] Followed by: Conversation 
[h2] The discussion is the most important part of Bridge. It’s a chance to interact with the talk and share your perspective in a safe environment. You can ask whatever question you like or not say a word. No one will ever call on you. ​
