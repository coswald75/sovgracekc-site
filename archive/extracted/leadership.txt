[h1] Meet the Team 
[h2] Our pastor loves the Lord, the Word of God, and the community in and around Providence Community Church. 
[h3] CHRIS OSWALD | Senior Pastor 
[p] Chris and his wife Angela have been married for 28 years and are grateful to have their three adult children (along with their spouses) over each Sunday for a Sabbath feast full of good food, laughter, and cut-throat card playing. When he gets a moment to himself, Chris loves listening to “Dad Rock” on his vintage hi-fi stereo. 
[h2] DOV COHEN | Elder 
[p] Dov and his wife Christine have been married for twelve years and enjoy raising their four young children. Second only to his time with the Lord, Dov cherishes his dates with Christine. He also loves to explore the Kansas City region on day trips with his whole family. When he’s not out and about with the fam – Dov is probably reading a book…or calling his parents just to say hello.
