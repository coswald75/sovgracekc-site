The Trip of a Lifetime!

Children in K-5th grade are welcome to join us for Zoomerang VBS at Redeemer Fellowship where we'll learn about Jesus in the land down under!

Independence School

Kindergarden -

AN UNFORGETTABLE WEEK OF FUN

Like a boomerang, we are returning kids to the value of life, from the lives of preborn babies to eternal life in the kingdom of God. You’ll marvel at the unforgettable wildlife, culture, and beauty of Australia while discovering that from the tiniest to the oldest, each person is made in the image of God—wonderfully designed to know him and live for him!

.

From the minute your mates step off the plane onto Australian soil, they will find themselves on a trip they’ll never forget! The day begins at the Aussie Assembly, the thrilling opening complete with entertaining welcomes, “aus-some” songs, a mission moment, and prayer. Then we're off to four fun rotation sites:

LESSONS

During the lesson time, our mates will unearth the value and wonder of all life created by God and for God.

TREATS

Mates will enjoy delicious “tucker” (food) at this much-anticipated stop.

GAMES

At this location, mates ‘av a go (put in a good effort) at fun land and sea challenges or have a blast working as teams and trying out various physical challenges.

SCIENCE & CRAFTS

Electrifying science experiments to explore, creative crafts to invent, high-energy songs to sing, fun memory verse games to play.
