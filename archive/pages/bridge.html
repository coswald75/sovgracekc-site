We All Have Questions

The Bridge Course is a way to explore the Christian faith in a relaxed, non-threatening environment over ten weeks.

Register for the Course

Conversations about the Big Questions of Life

Why does God allow suffering? Is Jesus the only way? Is Christianity really worth it? Each week there is a 30 minute talk designed to raise important questions and inspire conversation. After this, there’s a 30 minute discussion to interact with the talk and share your perspective. It’s a safe environment where you can ask whatever question you like or not say a word. No one will ever call on you. And the whole thing is free!

Register for the Course

Here's How it Works

First up: Food

Food has a way of bringing us together and creating good conversation. We start with food so we can get to know each other!

Next: Talk

Talks are designed to raise important questions and inspire conversation. Generally thirty minutes long, they seek to answer important questions.

Followed by: Conversation

The discussion is the most important part of Bridge. It’s a chance to interact with the talk and share your perspective in a safe environment. You can ask whatever question you like or not say a word. No one will ever call on you. ​
